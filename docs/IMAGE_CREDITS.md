## Nearby-place photos

`public/data/chennai-photo-places.json` contains a small attributed snapshot of real Chennai area/landmark records and exact Wikidata P18 / Commons image associations. Each image record includes the full author, title, source page and license URL; these are displayed in the map photo cards. Local files under `public/images/nearby/` are cropped only through CSS, with no change to the image content. They are not hotel or restaurant photos and are not shown outside their real discovery radius. Snapshot captured 2026-11-17; community coordinates and information can be incomplete or outdated.

Other areas use live nearby Wikipedia coordinates, explicit Wikidata images and Commons license metadata. Wikipedia text descriptions are attributed to their article. These third-party content licenses remain separate from MIT-licensed code.
